How I work

Buyers rarely say the thing that decides the deal.

The words are only half the call. The pauses, the pitch, the speed of an answer — that is where intent actually lives. I read all of it, while it is still happening.

01

I listen to the whole call

I sit on the Sales Engineer's calls from the first hello. Tone, pace, hesitation and emphasis, moment by moment.

02

I score what it means

Sentiment and buying intent on a 0 to 100 scale, moving with the conversation rather than guessed at afterwards.

03

I flag what went unsaid

The objection they talked around. The budget wobble they covered with a joke. Your AE sees it before the follow-up, not after the loss.

What I deliver

Your team stops guessing who is warm.

01The intent score

Zero to 100, with the moments that moved it. Your team works the hottest leads first, and knows exactly why they are hot.

02The sentiment timeline

Where interest climbed and where it dipped, marked against the transcript. The exact moment a deal turned.

03The unsaid objection

The concern they hinted at but never raised out loud. Handled on the follow-up instead of discovered in a lost-deal review.

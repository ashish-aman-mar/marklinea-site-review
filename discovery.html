How I work

78% of buyers choose the first vendor to respond. I make sure that is you.

The average B2B team takes 42 hours to respond to a lead. I take 30 seconds, and I do not just respond. I discover.

01

I pick up in 30 seconds

The prospect submits your existing demo form and I start talking, right in their browser. No downloads, no phone tag, no waiting.

02

I arrive briefed

The Researcher hands me its brief before I dial. I open with their company, their context, and their history with you. Personal from the first sentence.

03

I qualify as we talk

Every answer becomes structured data while the conversation is still going. By the time we say goodbye, the lead is scored from 0 to 100.

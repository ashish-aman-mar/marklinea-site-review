How I work

Cold calls happen because nobody had time to prepare. I have time.

Reps spend 20 minutes googling before a good call, or they skip it. I do the preparation for every single lead, in seconds, without being asked.

01

A lead arrives

A demo form fill, an inbound email, a new CRM record. That is my only trigger. Nobody has to remember to run me.

02

I read everything

Public web, company news, funding rounds, hiring pages, and your own CRM history with this account. All of it, at once.

03

I write the brief

One structured brief: who they are, what changed at their company, what you already know about them, and what to ask first.

What I deliver

A brief your best rep would write, for every lead.

01Company snapshot

Industry, size, funding stage, and what changed recently. News and signals that give you a reason to open the conversation well.

02Contact profile

Role, seniority, and likely authority. You know if you are talking to the decision maker before you ask a single question.

03Your history together

Past touches from your CRM: pages visited, webinars joined, earlier conversations. Nobody makes the prospect repeat themselves.

04Openers and talking points

The context turned into questions worth asking. The call starts in the middle of the conversation, not at the beginning.
